Therapies

Pain care without pills or surgery

After your assessment, the doctor chooses the therapies that suit your case. You may need one or a mix.

  • Neurotherapy

    Works on the nerves and muscles around the painful area.

  • Chiropractic care

    Hands-on spine and joint adjustments for back and neck problems.

  • Acupuncture

    Fine needles placed at selected points to ease pain and tension.

  • Cupping therapy

    Hot, vacuum and blood cupping for stiff muscles and sore backs.

  • Acupressure

    Focused pressure on specific points, with no needles.

  • Leech therapy and naturopathy

    Traditional options your doctor may suggest for certain joint conditions.

Questions

Before you book

Who will I see at the Nallagandla branch?

Dr. Amrita, a physiotherapist, alternative therapist, chiropractor and acupuncturist, is present at this branch.

Do I need an appointment?

An appointment keeps your wait short. Send the form above and we will confirm a time by phone.

Is the treatment drug-free?

Yes. Reset uses hands-on and traditional therapies, with no medicines and no surgery.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the cause and how long you have had the pain. The doctor tells you after the first assessment.

Can my family member come with me?

Yes. Bring anyone who can help you travel or who wants to understand the plan.
